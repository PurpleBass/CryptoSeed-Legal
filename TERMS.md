### 2. Description of the App

CryptoSeed is a local-only cryptography utility for iOS that provides tools to encrypt and decrypt text and files, manage cryptographic keys and identities, and perform related cryptographic operations entirely on the user’s device.

CryptoSeed includes:

* A user interface for encrypting and decrypting text and files
* Integration with Apple Shortcuts via App Intents
* Local management of symmetric keys and asymmetric identities

CryptoSeed:

* Does not require user accounts
* Does not operate servers
* Does not collect analytics or telemetry
* Does not monitor user activity
* Does not access, store, or transmit user data unless the user explicitly chooses to share it

All cryptographic operations occur locally on the user’s device.
